ABOUT

We make parts. We have for years.

HyperFab grew out of the workshop, not out of a slide deck. We have designed parts, built moulds, stood over production lines and pulled defective parts with our own hands. So when we look at your file, we know where it will break.

HyperFab’s technical core is three engineers: two Sharif University of Technology graduates in mechanical engineering (applied design) and a manufacturing engineering specialist. Together they cover mechanical design, forming-process simulation, controls, and production and quality management.

4polyethylene injection moulds, from build supervision to commissioning
80,000+parts produced with those moulds
8,000punched tubes in one industrial project, with quality-control responsibility
553D designs published by our design brand, Manabun Lab

Our experience is not limited to small parts. From injection moulds and deep-drawing dies for aluminium sheet to process simulation in Abaqus and AutoForm, we know the road from design to industrial production first-hand. Every order is backed by one responsible engineering team. You place the order with us once, and we choose the best way to make it, get your approval on a sample before production and inspect quality before delivery.

Make it precise, get it fast — with HyperFab.

WHY HYPERFAB

Why manufacture with HyperFab

Engineering before production

Before any machine starts, your file is checked for manufacturability, material, process and real dimensions. If a design cannot be made, we tell you why and what to change, up front.

Clear prices, realistic lead times

FDM printing and laser cutting are priced online, instantly. For other processes, price and lead time are fixed after review: no hidden costs, no “we’ll see later”.

Online tracking, nationwide shipping

Follow your order in your account; parts are packed securely and shipped anywhere in Iran.

HOW IT WORKS

How it works

From file to finished part in four steps.

1

You send the file

A 3D model, a technical drawing, or even a photo and a description, uploaded on hyperfab.ir.

2

An engineer reviews it

Manufacturability, material, process and real dimensions are checked.

3

Price and lead time are fixed

Instant online for FDM and laser; after review for everything else. No hidden costs.

4

We make and ship it

With inspection before packing and delivery anywhere in Iran.
