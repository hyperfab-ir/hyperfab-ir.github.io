WHAT IT IS

What powder coating is

Powder coating applies a dry powder to a metal part. The electrically charged powder clings to the part, which is then cured in an oven so the powder melts into a continuous, hard layer. The result is thicker, more even and more resistant to impact, scratches and corrosion than liquid paint.

HyperFab powder-coats batch metal parts with high quality at a fair price: machine housings and chassis, structures, industrial parts and consumer products. If the part is also made at HyperFab — by punching, welding or turning — you receive the coated, finished part in one place.

For production runs only: this service is for batch production; one-off parts are accepted only rarely. A suitable quantity is recommended after reviewing the project.
FINISHES

Powder coating finishes

Electrostatic powder

The most common powder coating: an even, solvent-free finish with strong adhesion and durability.

Pearl (metallic and pearlescent)

A metallic or pearlescent sheen for cosmetic parts, décor and premium products.

Hammer finish

A texture like hammered metal that hides imperfections; common on machine housings, cabinets and tools.

Texture and wrinkle

A rough, matt surface that hides fingerprints and scratches; for high-touch parts.

Gloss, satin and matt

The gloss level to suit the use and look of the product.

Wide colour range

A wide range of colours, including standard RAL codes.

PROCESS

Powder coating steps

1

Surface preparation

Degreasing and cleaning, plus other preparation where needed for better adhesion.

2

Powder application

Charged powder is sprayed evenly onto the part.

3

Oven curing

The part is cured in an oven so the powder melts into a hard, continuous layer.

4

Inspection and delivery

Appearance and evenness are checked and parts are packed and delivered.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is powder coating?
Powder coating is an electrostatically charged powder that clings to a metal part and is cured in an oven into a hard, even layer. It is more durable than liquid paint.
Which powder coatings does HyperFab offer?
Electrostatic powder, pearl (metallic and pearlescent), hammer, texture and wrinkle finishes, in gloss, satin or matt and a wide range of colours.
What is a pearl finish?
A powder coating with metallic or pearlescent particles that gives a metallic or pearl-like sheen; popular for cosmetic parts and premium products.
Do you powder-coat aluminium?
Yes. Powder coating is done on steel, iron, aluminium and other metals. On aluminium, chromate as a base improves paint adhesion.
Do you coat single parts?
The service is for production runs; one-off work is accepted only rarely.
How is powder coating priced?
By part size and surface area, finish type and texture, preparation required and quantity. The price is quoted after review.
