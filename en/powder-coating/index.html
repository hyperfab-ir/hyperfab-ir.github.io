WHAT IT IS

What is powder coating?

Powder coating applies a dry powder to a metal part. The electrically charged powder clings to the part, which is then cured in an oven so the powder melts into a continuous, hard layer. The result is thicker, more even and more resistant to impact, scratches and corrosion than liquid paint.

HyperFab powder-coats batch metal parts with high quality at a fair price: machine housings and chassis, structures, industrial parts and consumer products. If the part is also made at HyperFab — by punching, welding or turning — you receive the coated, finished part in one place. On aluminium, chromate conversion as a base improves paint adhesion.

For production runs only: this service is for batch production; one-off parts are accepted only rarely. A suitable quantity is recommended after reviewing the project.
FINISHES

Which powder coating finishes are available?

Electrostatic powder, pearl, hammer, texture and wrinkle finishes, in gloss, satin or matt and a wide range of colours, including standard RAL codes.

Electrostatic powder

The most common powder coating: an even, solvent-free finish with strong adhesion and durability.

Pearl (metallic and pearlescent)

A metallic or pearlescent sheen for cosmetic parts, décor and premium products.

Hammer finish

A texture like hammered metal that hides imperfections; common on machine housings, cabinets and tools.

Texture and wrinkle

A rough, matt surface that hides fingerprints and scratches; for high-touch parts.

Gloss, satin and matt

The gloss level to suit the use and look of the product.

Wide colour range

A wide range of colours, including standard RAL codes.

PROCESS

How does powder coating work, step by step?

Surface preparation, electrostatic powder application, oven curing, then inspection and delivery.

1

Surface preparation

Degreasing and cleaning, plus other preparation where needed for better adhesion.

2

Powder application

Charged powder is sprayed evenly onto the part.

3

Oven curing

The part is cured in an oven so the powder melts into a hard, continuous layer.

4

Inspection and delivery

Appearance and evenness are checked and parts are packed and delivered.
