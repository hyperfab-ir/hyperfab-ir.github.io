Sheet & Tube Punching

HyperFab in Tehran, Iran punches metal and polyethylene sheet and tube, builds the die and runs production. Priced as die cost plus cost per stroke.

Mould Making & Injection Moulding

HyperFab in Tehran, Iran designs and builds injection moulds and produces plastic parts in PE, PP, ABS and nylon. Priced as mould cost plus cost per shot.

Batch CNC Turning

HyperFab in Tehran, Iran produces batch CNC turned parts to drawing in brass, MO40 and CK45 steel, with fit tolerances and first-article approval.

Aluminium Extrusion

HyperFab in Tehran, Iran extrudes aluminium profiles in your own cross-section with a dedicated die. You order by tonnage and pay for the die.

Welding & Welded Structures

HyperFab in Tehran, Iran does TIG and MIG welding of stainless steel and 6000-series aluminium and welded structures to drawing, with VT, PT and PAUT.

Plating & Anodizing

HyperFab in Tehran, Iran does gold and silver plating on brass and other metals, and anodizing and chromate (Alodine) on aluminium, for production runs.

Powder Coating

HyperFab in Tehran, Iran powder-coats batch metal parts in pearl, hammer, textured, gloss and matt finishes. Priced by size, finish and quantity.

INDUSTRIAL PRODUCTION

Industrial and batch production: from moulds and turning to welding, plating and coating

A complete chain for batch parts, from production to final finish, with one responsible engineering team.

When a part moves from prototype to volume, the process has to change. For industrial and batch production, HyperFab offers a complete chain: sheet and tube punching, mould making and injection moulding, batch CNC turning, aluminium extrusion, welding and welded fabrication, plating and anodizing and powder coating. A part can go from production to final finish in one place.

HyperFab’s engineering team comes from Iran’s top universities, including Sharif University of Technology, and has worked in industry for years. It has taken four polyethylene injection moulds from build to commissioning, and more than 80,000 parts have been produced with them. Its record also includes quality control of 8,000 punched tubes, mating brass parts and MO40 and CK45 gauges, and stainless and aluminium structures for the medical and marine industries.

Sheet and tube punching: metal and polyethylene; punching die design and manufacture and production runs.

Mould making and injection moulding: mould making, sampling and production in PE, PP, ABS and nylon.

Batch CNC turning: brass parts with fit tolerances and gauge parts in MO40 and CK45.

Aluminium extrusion: your section with a dedicated die; you bring tonnage and die cost, we do the rest.

TIG and MIG welding: stainless steel and 6000-series aluminium, welded structures and platforms, with VT, PT and PAUT.

Plating and anodizing: silver and gold on brass and other metals, anodizing and chromate on aluminium.

Powder coating: powder, pearl, hammer and textured finishes.
