WHAT IT IS

What Tekeyab is and the problem it solves

Anyone who has built a part or repaired a machine knows the routine. For one brass bushing or a small aluminium plate, you call several suppliers. Each sells only six-metre lengths or full sheets and quotes prices over the phone. In the end you are still not sure the material is the grade you asked for. Buying a small piece of industrial material usually means call after call.

Tekeyab is HyperFab’s online service built for exactly this. You describe the piece once in Tekeyab’s four-step form: material and grade, shape, dimensions, quantity, when you need it and how it should be delivered. You can attach a photo or notes if needed. The HyperFab team asks its supplier network and posts the final price to your account. After payment, the piece is prepared, cut to size if needed and shipped.

Tekeyab is connected to HyperFab’s fabrication services. If the piece will be turned, CNC-milled, laser-cut or bent, you can order the fabrication in the same place. Material and manufacturing are handled together, and you don’t have to carry stock between supplier and workshop.

MATERIALS

What you can buy through Tekeyab

Six material families; if you need a specific grade or alloy, write it in the form.

Aluminium

Aluminium sheet, plate, bar, flat bar and profile; light and easy to machine for housings, mounting plates, fixtures and machined parts.

Stainless steel

Stainless sheet, bar and tube for parts in contact with water, food or chemicals.

Steel

Steel bar, shaft, flat bar and sheet for shafts, flanges, brackets and load-bearing parts.

Copper and brass

Free-machining brass for bushings, nozzles and fittings, and copper for electrical and thermal uses.

Engineering plastics

PTFE (Teflon), polyethylene, acetal (POM), nylon and acrylic for gaskets, bushings, gears, sliding and insulating parts.

Other and special metals

If you need a rare material or alloy, describe it and its availability will be checked.

TRACKING

Track your request with a TK code

Every Tekeyab request gets a tracking code such as TK1001 and appears under the “Material orders” tab of your HyperFab account. Its status updates there, from “sourcing” and “price quoted” to “payment confirmed”, “preparing”, “shipped” and “delivered”.

If the requested material cannot be found, you are told so clearly; requests are not left hanging. Tekeyab questions are answered by HyperFab’s industrial team on the Bale messenger (@hyperfab_pro).

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is HyperFab Tekeyab?
Tekeyab is HyperFab’s online service for buying small pieces of industrial material. You describe the piece in a form: material, shape, dimensions, quantity, when you need it and how to deliver it. HyperFab sources it from suppliers, quotes a price, cuts it to size if needed and ships it.
What materials can I buy through Tekeyab?
Aluminium, stainless steel, steel, copper and brass, engineering plastics such as PTFE, polyethylene, acetal (POM), nylon and acrylic, and special metals. Shapes include sheet and plate, shaft and round bar, flat bar, tube and profile.
What is the minimum quantity?
Tekeyab is built for small quantities, and you can request a single piece. The final price depends on material, dimensions and any cutting required.
Do you cut material to size?
Yes. The piece is cut to the requested size when needed. If it then needs machining, laser cutting or bending, you can order that fabrication from HyperFab as well.
When and how do I receive the price?
After sourcing from suppliers, the final price is posted to your HyperFab account and you are notified by email or messenger. You don’t need to call to get a price.
What do I need to submit a request?
Only the piece’s specifications and a mobile number. The number is verified by SMS code, and an account is created automatically so you can track the request.
Does Tekeyab deliver outside Tehran?
Yes. There are three delivery options: courier in Tehran, in-person pickup, and shipping to other cities.
What if the material cannot be found?
If material matching your specifications cannot be found, the request is marked “not found” and you are not charged. If a suitable alternative exists, it is suggested.
