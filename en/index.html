IN SHORT

HyperFab at a glance

HyperFab (Persian: هایپرفب) is an online manufacturing platform based in Tehran, Iran. HyperFab offers FDM and resin 3D printing, CNC machining, metal and non-metal laser cutting and sheet metal bending online. Customers upload a design file on hyperfab.ir. For FDM 3D printing and laser cutting, the online price estimator gives a price instantly. For other processes, an engineer reviews the file and quotes a price.

HyperFab’s Tekeyab service sources small pieces of industrial material, quotes a price and cuts them to size if needed. The materials are sheet, bar, shaft, flat bar, tube and profile in aluminium, stainless steel, steel, copper, brass and engineering plastics. Most orders are ready within three working days and the minimum order is one part. Shipping across Iran is by Post, Tipax and Snappbox, and pickup in Tehran is free.

Online price estimator: the price of your part, instantly

3D printing prices from STL and 3MF files, laser cutting and bending prices from DXF files — no phone quotes.

The HyperFab price estimator (HyperFab Quote) is the online tool on hyperfab.ir that prices a manufactured part. You upload the part file, choose material, thickness, scale and quantity, and see the production price immediately, with no phone calls, no email back-and-forth and no waiting days for a quote.

It has two calculation engines: an FDM 3D printing engine that analyses STL and 3MF files, and a laser cutting and sheet bending engine that reads DXF files. Both run inside your own browser. To get a price, your file is not sent to any server; it is uploaded only when you finalise the order, and only to make that order.

FDM 3D printing: optimal part orientation, support analysis, wall and infill volume slice by slice, filament mass and print time.

Laser cutting and bending: cut length, pierce count, number and length of bends, engraving and sheet weight.

Automatic drawing checks: duplicate lines, open contours, holes smaller than the thickness, holes too close to a bend line and more than ten other common errors, before you pay.

File privacy: everything is calculated in your browser; the file is uploaded only when you finalise the order.

FDM 3D Printing

FDM (Fused Deposition Modeling) 3D printing at HyperFab turns your 3D file into a plastic part, layer by layer, with no mould or tooling. You upload an STL or 3MF file on hyperfab.ir and the online price estimator instantly shows volume, weight, dimensions, print time and price. The calculation runs in your own browser, so the file is not sent anywhere until you place the order. PLA, PETG, ABS and TPU filaments are available, with layer heights from 0.1 to 0.3 mm.

FDM is the most economical additive manufacturing process and gives the best cost-to-result ratio for prototypes, spare parts that are no longer sold, workshop jigs and fixtures, architectural models and student projects. Before printing, every model is checked for wall thickness, orientation and weak points. The minimum order is a single part, quantity discounts are applied automatically, and parts are ready within three working days and shipped anywhere in Iran.

Resin 3D Printing (SLA / DLP)

Resin 3D printing (SLA / DLP) cures liquid resin layer by layer with ultraviolet light, giving far finer detail and a much smoother surface than FDM. HyperFab’s resin printer uses a 16K screen with roughly 14 × 19 micron horizontal resolution, and layer height is adjustable from 0.02 to 0.1 mm, so layer lines are almost invisible.

It is the first choice for small, precise parts: figures and miniatures, dental models, jewellery casting patterns, high-detail prototypes and display models. Three resins are available: standard resin for the highest detail, a tough engineering resin for parts that must take some impact, and a castable resin for investment-casting patterns. Resin print prices are based on resin volume and print time and are quoted after the file has been reviewed.

CNC Machining

CNC machining at HyperFab covers milling, turning, drilling, slotting and threading to your drawing, in engineering plastics and soft metals: polyethylene and acetal (POM / PE), PTFE (Teflon), nylon (PA6), acrylic (PMMA), Delrin (POM-C) and brass. Standard tolerance is ±0.05 mm, tighter tolerances are possible by arrangement, and surface finish is Ra 1.6 or better depending on material and tooling.

You upload a STEP or IGES model, a DWG or DXF drawing, or a dimensioned PDF on hyperfab.ir. The drawing is reviewed for tolerances, tool access to corners and manufacturability, then a price and lead time are quoted. Typical jobs include bushings, gears and rollers for machinery, PTFE seals and gaskets for chemical equipment, jigs and fixtures, clear acrylic enclosures and decorative brass parts. The minimum order is one part and lead time is up to three working days, depending on complexity.

Metal Laser Cutting

HyperFab cuts mild steel, stainless steel, aluminium, galvanised and oiled sheet on a fibre laser with nitrogen assist gas, leaving a clean, oxide-free edge; CO₂ cutting is also available for general work. Cutting accuracy is ±0.1 mm with a kerf of about 0.15 mm, so outlines and hole positions match your file exactly and repeat from part to part.

Upload a DXF file on hyperfab.ir and the laser price estimator measures cut length, pierce count, sheet weight and bends directly from the drawing and gives a price instantly. At the same time it checks the drawing for duplicate lines, open contours, holes smaller than the sheet thickness and more than ten other common errors. Typical parts include brackets, machine panels and chassis, metal signs and logos, washers and plates, and sheet-metal prototypes. If the part also needs bending, cutting and bending are handled in one order.

Non-metal Laser Cutting & Engraving

HyperFab cuts and engraves acrylic (Plexiglas), MDF, wood, leather, felt and foam on a CO₂ laser. Through-cutting, surface engraving and marking can be combined in one file and one run, and delicate lattice designs that a saw or router cannot reach are cut cleanly. Cutting accuracy is ±0.1 mm.

Send a vector DXF at 1:1 scale in millimetres, with cut lines and engraving areas on separate colours or layers. Typical jobs include custom boxes and packaging, architectural and teaching models, acrylic signs and logos, promotional items such as key rings and coasters, fabric and leather templates, and decorative panels. Single parts are welcome, and for larger runs the nesting on the sheet is optimised to lower the unit price.

Sheet Metal Bending

HyperFab bends sheet metal on a press brake with V-dies (air bending) to an angular accuracy of ±1°. Bending can be ordered on its own or as the next step after laser cutting. When cutting and bending happen in one place, the part does not travel between workshops, lead time is shorter and bend positions line up more accurately with the holes.

Send the flat pattern as a DXF, a 3D STEP model or a dimensioned PDF; from a 3D model the correct flat pattern is calculated with the K-factor. Bend sequence, bend radius and tool clearance are checked before production so the sheet does not crack at the bend. Typical parts include L and U brackets, electrical cabinets and machine enclosures, chassis and frames for industrial equipment, cable trays and channels, façade panels and sheet-metal spare parts.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is HyperFab?
HyperFab (هایپرفب) is an online manufacturing platform in Tehran, Iran, offering FDM and resin 3D printing, CNC machining, metal and non-metal laser cutting and sheet metal bending. HyperFab also runs an instant online price estimator and the Tekeyab service for buying small pieces of industrial material. The main website and ordering are at hyperfab.ir.
How do I get an online 3D printing price?
On hyperfab.ir, upload an STL or 3MF file in the order section. HyperFab’s estimator analyses the model in your browser and instantly shows the FDM price based on material, infill, layer height, scale and quantity. Resin printing is quoted after file review.
How do I get a laser cutting price?
Upload the part’s DXF file at 1:1 scale in millimetres on hyperfab.ir and choose sheet material and thickness. The estimator measures cut length, pierce count, bends and sheet weight from the drawing and gives a price instantly.
What is HyperFab Tekeyab?
Tekeyab is a service for buying small pieces of industrial material. You describe the piece in a form: material, shape, dimensions, quantity, when you need it and how to deliver it. HyperFab sources it from suppliers, quotes a price, cuts it to size if needed and ships it. Shapes include sheet, bar, shaft, flat bar, tube and profile, and materials include aluminium, stainless steel, steel, copper, brass and engineering plastics.
How long does manufacturing take?
Most orders are ready within three working days. The exact time depends on the process, size and quantity. Shipping time is added to this and depends on the destination city and shipping method.
Is there a minimum order quantity?
No. Even a single part is made. For larger quantities, a stepped discount is applied to the unit price automatically.
Does HyperFab ship outside Tehran?
Yes, anywhere in Iran via Post, Tipax and Snappbox. Pickup in Tehran is free.
I don’t have a design file. What can I do?
Send a hand sketch, a photo of a sample part or a description of what it does. Design and 3D modelling are offered at a separate cost, and production starts after you approve the design. The order form also has an “I’m not sure / I want advice” option.
