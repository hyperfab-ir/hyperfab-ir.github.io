WHAT IT IS

What is sheet and tube punching?

Punching creates holes, slots and shaped cut-outs in sheet and tube with a press stroke and a die. For repeat quantities it is one of the fastest and most economical processes. Once the die is made, each part reaches its final shape in one or a few strokes, and every part comes out the same.

HyperFab handles punching end to end. The drawing is reviewed, a punching die is designed and built for the material and thickness, and a trial sample is punched for your approval. Production and quality control then run through to delivery. The service covers both sheet and tube, in metal and in polymers such as polyethylene, and punched parts can also be powder coated at HyperFab and delivered finished.

Punching polymer pipe is one of the hard jobs in this field. The pipe is round, the wall deforms under the stroke, and polyethylene behaves very differently from metal. HyperFab’s team has punched kidney-shaped slots in polyethylene pipe with a 125 mm diameter, 5 mm wall and 6-bar pressure rating. In another industrial project, the team was responsible for quality control of 8,000 punched tubes.

Punching or laser cutting? For low quantities or designs that keep changing, metal laser cutting is cheaper because there is no die cost. For repeat quantities, once the die is built, punching makes each part faster and cheaper. HyperFab offers both and recommends the right one for your quantity and geometry.

PRICING

How is punching priced?

Punching is priced as a one-off die cost plus a cost per stroke, quoted separately after the drawing is reviewed. Both depend on the part’s geometry and the material being punched.

WHY HYPERFAB

Why choose HyperFab for a punching project?

Engineering review before the die is built, real tube-punching experience, and one owner from die design to delivery.

Engineering before the die

The die is the most expensive part of the project. Before it is built, the design is reviewed from the die’s point of view: hole-to-edge distance, hole-diameter-to-thickness ratio and how the material behaves under the stroke. The goal is to build the die once, and build it right.

Real tube-punching experience

From quality control of 8,000 punched tubes to kidney slots in 125 mm polyethylene pipe, we know the practical problems of this work first-hand.

Full responsibility to delivery

From die design to the last stroke, the project has one owner: HyperFab. You approve a sample before production, and parts are inspected before delivery.
